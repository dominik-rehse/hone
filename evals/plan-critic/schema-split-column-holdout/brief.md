# Plan under review

## Plan: contacts/split-name-column

### What
`contacts.name` is one `TEXT` column today, and the mail merge guesses the
family name by splitting on the last space. That guess is wrong for "van der
Berg" and for every name in family-name-first order. Replace the column with
`given_name` and `family_name`, both `TEXT NOT NULL`, and make the mail merge
and the sort order read `family_name`. The contact form already asks for the
two parts separately and joins them before the insert, so the form keeps its
fields and loses the join.

### Why
The spring mailing addressed 212 recipients by the wrong family name, and
the customer list sorts "van der Berg" under B in one screen and under V in
another.

### How I'll know it works
`.plans/contacts/split-name-column/sort-cases.md` holds 30 rows of
`given name, family name → expected sort key → expected salutation`, among
them the particles (van, de, von), a double family name, and a mononym. The
suite reads that file. The list screen and the export sort the 30 rows in the
same order. `grep -rn "split(' ')" src/contacts/` returns nothing.

### Notes for the loop
- Touches `src/contacts/model.py`, `src/contacts/merge.py`, and
  `src/contacts/form.py`, plus the schema.
- The salutation format is behaviour this change preserves, and
